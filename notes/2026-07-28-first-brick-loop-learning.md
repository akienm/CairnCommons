# The day both bricks earned their loops

*2026-07-28. Record kept at Akien's word: "be shure to leave good notes, we'll want
this to show up when we write about this project."*

## What a stackable learning brick is

A **stackable learning brick** (Akien's name, ratified this day; the earlier records
say *question nexus*) is a component whose learning path has one shape: a
deterministic **floor**, a walkable **tree** of its own prior learnings, and an LLM
**ceiling** that drafts — with the rule that nothing the ceiling drafts becomes
fire-path behavior except by **installation**: dated-failure provenance, ratification,
a proof tooth. Fire-paths are hardware; the tree is never consulted at verdict time.
Bricks stack: the ladder's rungs stack vertically, and the bricks compose into the
/chart chain horizontally.

Until this day the shape was proven but unexercised — the loops existed, seeded with
founding scars, and had never carried a *new* learning end to end.

## The morning: the inspector's brick gets its first subject

Akien ordered the constrain work as a **higher-order build-the-test-first**: "packet-
inspector first, and for constraints, we set up it's inspector filters first. then
build the constraints module." The judges (`constraint_traces`,
`constraint_bounds_complete`) were installed and sealed in the build_inspector — with
provenance from failures that *predate* the module (the fabricated-attribution class,
2026-07-26; the carrier miss, 2026-07-28) — before `constrain.py` existed. The module
then grew into judges it did not write and structurally cannot modify (it imports the
judge; the judge's owner never imports back). Its first real packet passed at the
berth door and again at promotion — one implementation, two mouths, identity-pinned.

## The evening: orient's brick gets its first correction

During the constrain build, one proof red fired that had fired once before, the same
day: an import-allowlist tooth refused `from cairn.chart import constrain` because it
recorded the *spelling* (the AST prefix `cairn.chart`) rather than the *capability*
(the module actually entering, `cairn.chart.constrain` — identical either way Python
spells it). The fix applied both times was a respelling: treating the symptom.

Akien asked: **"how do we address this?"** — and that question was the learning event.

1. **Measure before deciding.** A one-shot scan found **70 loose-form imports
   repo-wide** — the loose spelling is the house idiom, not a defect. This *refuted*
   the reflex answer (a gate filter banning the form would red seventy healthy sites).
2. **Deposit.** The correction entered orient's corrections corpus, dated, naming
   both reds (node `cd9e57c05b35661b`).
3. **The walk did diagnostic work.** The proposal's counsel ranked the corpus's
   echo-label scar (0.6704) and emit-homonym scar (0.6532) nearest — both
   *word-not-capability* failures. The tree said "third member of a known family"
   before any human framed it that way, and that framing shaped the fix.
4. **Propose.** `propose_scan` assembled the artifact — content, walk, and the
   admission gate traveling inside it ("installation only... tooth 12 refuses a scan
   nobody was taught by... ratified by Akien").
5. **Ratify.** Akien: "i like it."
6. **Install.** `import_map` joined orient's SCANS: the capability-measured import
   list of a file, loose forms resolved through the filesystem. Tooth 16 pins three
   spellings of one module measuring as one capability. Six proofs that carried
   hand-rolled import walkers — themselves the compose-don't-parallel violation,
   invisible until the scan existed — recomposed over it. **Every allowlist survived
   unchanged**, green before and after: the resolution matched truth everywhere.

## Why this day matters for the record

- The machinery built in the morning carried its first real learning by evening —
  in both directions at once: the inspector's brick grew by a *pre-installed
  judgment* (test-first), orient's by a *measured correction* (failure-first). Same
  shape, opposite entry points.
- The anti-puppet claim stopped being rhetoric the same day: the dial moved off its
  all-claude baseline (orient 0.133 floor / 0.067 tree / 0.80 claude; constrain
  *born* at 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.25 because its floor surfaces charter text verbatim).
- The loop's key property held under real load: **nothing the ceiling drafted became
  behavior without passing a gate someone else owns.** The corpus advised, the
  measurement decided, the human ratified, the installation carried its provenance,
  and the proof teeth now hold the line without anyone remembering to.
